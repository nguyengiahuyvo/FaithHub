# FaithHub - User Documentation

**Your community. Your faith. One app.**

FaithHub is a mobile app designed for churches, prayer groups, and faith-based communities. It brings your community together with shared tasks, events, and member management — all in one place.

Available on **iOS**, **Android**, and **Web**.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Open FaithHub and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **display name**, **email address**, and choose a **password** (minimum 6 characters).
3. Confirm your password and tap **Sign Up**.

You can also sign in with **Apple** on iOS devices for a faster setup.

### Signing In

If you already have an account, simply enter your email and password on the login screen and tap **Sign In**.

---

## Joining Your Community

When you first sign in, you'll be asked to join an organization (your church, group, or community).

1. Ask your community leader for the **organization code** (e.g., `GRACE-2024`).
2. Enter the code on the Home screen.
3. Tap **Join** — you're in!

Once you join, you'll have access to your community's shared tasks, calendar, and member directory.

## Home Dashboard

The Home screen is your community hub. Here you'll find:

- A **personalized welcome** greeting.
- Your **organization name** displayed at the top.
- A **members list** showing everyone in your community, including their name, email, and role.

This is a quick way to see who's part of your group and stay connected.

## Tasks

The Tasks tab helps your community stay organized by managing shared to-dos.

### Creating a Task

1. Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner.
2. Enter a **title** (required) and an optional **description**.
3. Tap **Create**.

The task will be visible to all members of your organization.

### Managing Tasks

- **Mark as done:** Tap the checkbox next to any task to mark it as complete. Tap again to undo.
- **Delete a task:** If you created the task, tap the trash icon to remove it. You'll be asked to confirm before it's deleted.

Tasks are sorted so incomplete items always appear at the top.

## Calendar & Events

The Calendar tab lets your community plan and track events together.

### Browsing the Calendar

- Swipe or tap the **arrows** to navigate between months.
- Dates with events are marked with a **green dot**.
- Tap any date to see the events scheduled for that day.

### Creating an Event

1. Tap the **+** button.
2. Fill in the **event name** (required).
3. The **date** is pre-filled with your selected date, but you can change it.
4. Optionally add a **time** and **description**.
5. Tap **Create**.

### Attending Events

- Each event shows an **attendee count** and avatar bubbles of people who are going.
- Tap **Attend** to let others know you'll be there. The button changes to **Attending** (highlighted in green).
- Tap again to remove yourself from the attendee list.

### Deleting an Event

If you created the event, you can delete it by tapping the trash icon on the event card.

## Profile & Settings

The Profile tab is where you manage your account and preferences.

### Account Information

View your:

- **Display name**
- **Email address**
- **Account ID**

### Organization

If you're part of a community, you'll see:

- Your **organization name**
- Your **role** within the organization
- An option to **leave the organization**

### Preferences

- **Language:** Choose between English, Deutsch (German), or Tieng Viet (Vietnamese). Your preference is saved and applied across the entire app.
- **Notifications** and **Appearance** settings are coming soon.

### Signing Out

Tap **Sign Out** at the bottom of the profile screen. You'll be asked to confirm before being signed out.

## Language Support

FaithHub is available in three languages:

| Language   | Label      |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| English    | English    |
| German     | Deutsch    |
| Vietnamese | Tieng Viet |

To change your language:

1. Go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Under **Preferences**, tap your preferred language.
3. The entire app updates instantly.

Your language preference is saved to your account, so it stays the same across devices.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I get an organization code?**
A: Ask your church leader, pastor, or group admin. They create the organization and provide the code for members to join.

**Q: Can I be in multiple organizations?**
A: Currently, FaithHub supports one organization per account.

**Q: Who can delete tasks and events?**
A: Only the person who created a task or event can delete it.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Tasks and events are shared within your organization. Only members of your community can see them.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Contact your app administrator for account recovery assistance.

**Q: Can I use FaithHub without joining an organization?**
A: You can sign in and access your profile, but the Tasks and Calendar features require an organization membership.
